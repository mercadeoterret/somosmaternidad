Somos Maternidad Publisher

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026 · Contact: somosmaternidadbrand@gmail.com

1. Summary

Somos Maternidad Publisher is an internal tool used only by the Somos Maternidad team to publish its own videos to its own TikTok account. It does not collect data from the public, does not sell data, and does not share data with third parties.

2. Data we receive from TikTok

  • Basic profile (scope user.info.basic): the connected account's open ID and display name, used only to show which account is connected.
  • Access and refresh tokens: issued by TikTok when the owner signs in, used only to upload and publish the brand's videos (scopes video.upload and video.publish).

We do not access followers, messages, other users' content, or any data beyond the above.

3. How data is stored

Tokens and profile data are stored only on the Somos Maternidad owner's computer, in the app's local data folder. They are not sent to any server operated by us. Videos are sent directly from that computer to TikTok through TikTok's official API.

4. How long we keep it

Data is kept while the TikTok account stays connected. Disconnecting the account in the app deletes its tokens. Access can also be revoked from TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions).

5. Children

The app is not directed to children and is not used by anyone outside the Somos Maternidad team.

6. Your rights and contact

To ask about or request deletion of any data related to this app, write to somosmaternidadbrand@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days.

7. Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above shows the latest version.
